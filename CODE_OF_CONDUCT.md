# Community code of conduct

Devdevian welcomes developers with different backgrounds, experience levels, and technology choices. Help make technical conversation useful and approachable.

- Discuss ideas and code respectfully; criticize the implementation without attacking the person.
- Explain context, disclose conflicts of interest, and credit others’ work.
- Do not harass, threaten, discriminate, impersonate, expose personal information, or post spam or malicious content.
- Respect privacy and community membership boundaries. Do not copy private reports into public issues.
- Follow maintainer guidance during issue and pull-request review.

For a post in the hosted forum, use its report action. Community owners and validators handle their communities; platform moderators handle the public feed. For conduct in this repository or a sensitive concern, contact **dv842449@gmail.com**. Security vulnerabilities follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

Maintainers may remove harmful content, close or restrict discussions, or limit participation to protect the community. They will consider the context and severity of a report. This policy applies to repository issues, pull requests, and project-managed community spaces.
